## Known limitations

- **Enter on a resize separator** acts on the panel before it (the library's primary panel), so
  on "Resize inspector" it affects the canvas. Use the arrow keys or Home/End. Pre-existing, not
  changed.
- **Centring on a large schema:** selecting from the list on the 2,000-table fixture centres from
  zoom 0.05 to 1 with one long frame. It is 160–600 ms with neighbourhood Off as well (baseline),
  and was 383–718 ms with 1 hop: semi-transparent tables cost more to paint while the view moves.
  Noted for P2-T10.
- Dimming is not shown in the table list or the minimap. Depth isn't persisted (P3-T01). No hover
  preview; both directions only; at most 2 hops.

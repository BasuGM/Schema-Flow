**Context.** SchemaFlow is a browser-only database schema explorer: it shows a schema as an
interactive entity-relationship diagram (table list · canvas · inspector). Your round-3 report
confirmed QA-004 fixed in production and raised **QA-005**: hover and click edge labels shrank with

- **QA-005:** edge labels are now counter-scaled, so they keep a constant on-screen size (about
  11 px monospace text) at any zoom. They stay anchored about 12 px beside the FK row they leave
  from, above tables.
- **Product decision:** selecting a table (canvas click, list click, or Enter on a focused node,
  including re-selecting the table that is already selected) clears any clicked edge and its pinned
  label.
- **Distinct selected-edge style:**
  - a **clicked** edge is a 3 px accent line with a soft halo;
  - **highlighted** edges (touching the selected table, or hovered) are 2 px accent lines without
    a halo;
  - other edges are 1.25 px grey.
  - DOM: `data-testid="edge-<relationshipId>"` carries `data-edge-state="default|highlighted|selected"`.
